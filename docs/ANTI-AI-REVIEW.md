# Review after user feedback: generic AI appearance

Sources read:
- Anthropic frontend-design guidance: https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/blob/main/plugins/frontend-design/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md
- NN/g, AI Prototyping in Real Design Contexts: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-prototyping/

These are design guidance and observed tendencies, not objective tests of whether something was generated. A cream background, rounded control or large heading is not inherently wrong. Context and information hierarchy decide whether it belongs.

## Findings in version 1
- Several interchangeable slogans explained little: “Ближче до музики”, “Сцена починається з людей”, “Це відчувається”, “Для того, що буде далі”.
- Heading style repeated at almost every section: heavy weight, tightly compressed spacing, very large size.
- Decoration without specific purpose: sound bars, star, rotated photo frame, shadows, vertical English label, diagonal arrows on nearly every action.
- Hero/about/gallery/cooperation/final CTA repeated a generic landing progression; the page was longer than the verified material justified.
- Role choice hid useful interaction behind an accordion even though visitors just needed to choose a contact reason.

## Revision
- Use the supplied YAR mark as the large hero identity. Separate it from the video instead of layering stock phrases over a dark gradient.
- Remove repetitive mission statements. Keep short factual labels: agency, photographs, collaboration.
- Show a photographic sequence at different sizes with one lead image and a real seven-image contact sheet. Preserve frames and subjects; no fabricated case titles.
- Three direct collaboration choices open the matching brief. Avoid a decorative accordion stage.
- Remove cut-corner buttons, floating circular arrows, hover zoom, fake polaroid rotation, star and ambient decorative marks.
- Retain the real cream/burgundy/ink palette and original mouth emblem. Do not replace brand colors with a generic anti-AI aesthetic.
- Keep the same gallery, keyboard dialog behavior, video controls, responsive photographs and honest local brief download.

Remaining limit: proper company positioning and service details require company answers. No search can create verified project case studies or legitimate company claims from an atmospheric photo set.
