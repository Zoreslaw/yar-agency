# YAR — на відстані звуку

Working direction: a close, human view of independent live music. Quiet monochrome backstage opening, then real color photographs, a cream editorial spread, burgundy collaboration section and the original mouth emblem. The page should feel like entering the room with the band.

## Design choices
- #1D223D ink, #991A1E burgundy, #FFFBD4 cream, #FFCC00 yellow. Supplied PNG appears more saturated blue than the moodboard HEX; use the declared HEX until clarified.
- Manrope for Ukrainian headings/body (temporary open-license substitute until actual licensed Gotham files are provided). League Spartan for Latin display.
- Oversized close-set headings, wide photographs, one deliberate overlapping emblem. No interchangeable service-card grid or endless decorative motion.
- Video: existing 24s montage with hard cuts, no clips 4/7 or strobes. Pause, offscreen suspension and reduced-motion support.
- Primary visitor route: understand music agency → see atmosphere → choose collaboration reason → prepare a brief.
- Treat supplied images as atmosphere until event names, dates, credits and YAR's role are verified. Never label them invented projects.
- No invented dates, roster, metrics, addresses or email. Contact prototype produces a local downloadable brief, does not pretend to send messages.
